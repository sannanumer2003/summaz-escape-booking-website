Make the Most of Your Day

The Classic Package gets you on the water. These add-ons keep you there longer, get you the footage, and take the rest of the trip off your plate. Everything below is arranged and paid for on-site. Our team will sort you out when you arrive.

Big Mable Tube Ride

Hold on tight. The Mable tube pulls you across the bay at whatever speed you can handle. Roughly 10 to 12 minutes of getting thrown around with your group. Families, couples, friend groups, it works for all of them.

Up to 6 riders per run so your whole crew goes together. Kids 7 and up can join.

The details

  • $45 per person
  • 2 to 6 riders per run
  • Ages 7 and up
  • 600 lb combined weight limit across all riders
  • Arranged with our team on the day

Drone Photos and Videos

Jet ski rides, the tube run, beach shots, anything happening on the water. You tell us what you want captured. The drone is out every day, no pre-booking needed, just let our team know when you arrive.

The details

  • $45 flat, covers photos and video
  • Same-day delivery to your phone
  • Available every day
  • Dedicated staff member, not a side job
