Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what personal data Summaz Escape collects when you make a booking through booking.summazescape.com, why we collect it, and how we use it.

1. What We Collect

When you make a booking, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your group size and visit date
  • Any special requirements or notes you provide

When you visit the site, we may collect your IP address, browser type, and pages visited through standard server logs.

2. Why We Collect It

We use your information to:

  • Process and confirm your booking
  • Send you a booking confirmation email
  • Contact you if any changes are made to your booking
  • Respond to queries before or after your visit
  • Meet legal and record-keeping obligations

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it to any third party.

3. Payments

Payments are made externally via Zelle or Venmo, or in person by card on the day. We do not collect or store any card numbers or payment credentials on our servers. No payment data passes through this site.

4. Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for the booking form to function. No advertising or tracking cookies are used. Disabling cookies in your browser settings may affect the booking experience.

5. Data Sharing

Your personal data is not sold or shared with third parties except where necessary to send your confirmation email or meet legal requirements.

6. Data Retention

Booking records including your name, contact details, and booking information are retained for a minimum of 5 years for compliance purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to your personal data, ask for corrections, or request deletion where legally permissible. Contact us at booking@summazescape.com to make a request.

8. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at booking@summazescape.com or +1 (242) 817-5387.
